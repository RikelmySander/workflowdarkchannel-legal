Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkFlowDarkChannel (including TiktokWorkFlowGTA6) handles information when you connect your YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram accounts.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the application via OAuth 2.0, we only request the minimum scopes required to identify your account and upload videos you explicitly approve (such as user.info.basic and video.upload on TikTok, or youtube.upload on YouTube).

2. Local Storage & Data Handling

OAuth access tokens, video files, and metadata are stored locally on the operator's own machine. We do not sell, rent, share, or transmit your personal data or OAuth tokens to third-party advertisers or external servers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Tokens remain stored locally only while the integration is active. You may delete local tokens or revoke access at any time via your TikTok or Google account permissions page, or request deletion by emailing radarvicecitybrasil@gmail.com.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact radarvicecitybrasil@gmail.com.
